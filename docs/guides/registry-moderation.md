# Registry moderation: a guide for moderators

Thank you for helping look after the Registry. This guide is for Registry
moderators: members the site owner has asked to keep the Registry tidy.
It says what you can do, how the Registry Desk works, and the few rules
that keep everyone's horses safe.

**Draft for the owner to approve** (Registry Desk Phase 1, Release 3).

---

## The short version

- Everything you change is **recorded with your name**, and **any
  moderator can undo it for 30 days** (from History, or the Undo on the
  message that appears when you save or remove). You can't break anything
  for good.
- **Never decide your own suggestion.** You see your own in the Queue, but
  its buttons are off; another moderator, or the owner, decides it. (The
  owner is the one exception: as the last word, the owner may decide their
  own.)
- **Merge, don't remove,** anything a member's horse, want list or set
  points at. You can only remove a row nothing points at.
- When the Registry's style isn't settled, **propose a rule** on the Rules
  tab instead of fixing it your own way.
- Not sure? **Leave it, and ask.** A row left alone for a day costs nothing.

## The Registry Desk

Open it with the **Registry Desk** button at the top of the Registry page
(`/catalog`), or go to `/catalog/desk`. Only moderators and the owner see
the button. Every reference page also has an **Edit in the Desk** link at
the bottom, for moderators and the owner only, which opens that entry in
the Desk's editor. The Desk has five tabs; the numbers on the tabs say how
many suggestions wait in the Queue and how many rules wait for agreement,
and a note at the top says how many merges wait for a second look. This
guide is on the Desk too: **Read the moderators' guide**, under the title.

### Queue

Members' suggestions, in five parts:

- **Corrections**, **New entries** and **Duplicates**: waiting for a
  decision. For each one:
    - **Approve** when it is right.
    - **Edit, then approve** when it is nearly right (a typo, a missing
      capital). You can also **add a field** the member didn't fill in: for
      "#5038 Morgan Mare", change the name to "Morgan Mare" and add the
      Model number 5038. The member is told what you changed. Fixing good
      work is better than turning it away over a capital letter.
    - **Say no, with a reason** when it is wrong. The member always hears
      why, so write it kindly and plainly: "The 1998 catalog lists this as
      bay, not chestnut" rather than "Wrong".
- **Applied by curators**: changes trusted members (Silver and Gold
  curators) made in the last 7 days, already live without anyone
  approving them. Look them over; if one is wrong, **Put it back ...**
  with a reason. The member is told why, and anything changed again since
  is left alone.
- **Second looks**: merges another moderator asked for. **Confirm the
  merge** if it is right, or **Say no, with a reason**. Nobody confirms
  their own merge.

**Holds.** Pressing **Edit, then approve** holds the suggestion for you for
30 minutes, so two moderators don't answer the same one; the card says who
has it and until when, and the hold lets go when you cancel or decide.
After 30 minutes anyone can pick it up. (Before the owner adds update 242
there are no holds.)

If a member changes their suggestion while you are looking at it, nothing
is decided: the card says so and offers **Reload the Queue**, so you
decide what they sent now. If the Registry has to leave a value out of a
new entry (a run type it doesn't know, say), the card lists what was left
out before it goes, so you can fix it on the entry.

If you made a piece (as an artist), another moderator decides suggestions
about it.

A duplicate you approve waits for a **second look**: another moderator or
the owner confirms the merge before it happens.

### Mold view

Pick a mold, then click through its releases one by one. The release's
full record opens below, and **every field can be edited**: the usual
ones, anything else the entry carries, and, under **Everything else**,
**Add a field** for any field the Registry keeps that isn't showing yet.
Resins, tack, props and dioramas show their own fields (edition size,
cast in, tack type, materials ...) from the start. Fields that hold a list
(materials, working parts) take the values separated by commas.

Save with the **Save** button or Ctrl+S (⌘S on a Mac); **J** and **K**, or
**Prev** and **Next**, move to the next and previous release. The keys rest
while a dialog is open. If a value can't be saved, the box is marked in red
with the reason under it, and your cursor goes to it.

Entries that aren't under a mold (a release nobody has filed yet, an
artist resin, tack, a prop) are found with **Find any entry, by name or
model number** on the Mold view's first page, or with **Edit in the Desk**
on their reference page.

**Unsaved changes.** You can change several releases before saving: each
one keeps its changes while you move around, and the list marks it with
an amber **dot**. If you follow a link or press Back with anything unsaved,
the Desk asks **Leave without saving N releases?**: **Stay**, **Leave
without saving**, or **Save all and leave**. Closing or reloading the tab
asks too (that one is the browser's own question).

**"Someone saved this while you were editing it."** If another moderator
saved the same release after you opened it, your save stops and nothing of
yours is written. The Desk shows what they saved next to what you typed.
**Keep mine and save** saves your boxes over theirs; **Use theirs, drop
mine** takes their version and forgets your changes.

Only releases filed **under this mold** show here. A set piece filed under
another mold (because the set names this mold) is edited from its own mold.

**Needs work** shows only the releases with something to look at. When you
save one there, it stays in the list until you move on, and **Next** carries
on from it. A to-do list opens its rows this way, with **Back to the list**
at the top.

From the Mold view you can also:

- **Move a release to another mold** (re-file it). Its scale follows the
  new mold. Members' horses stay linked: they point at the release, not
  the mold. **Take it off this mold** asks first.
- **Merge** two releases that are the same thing: tick them (on a phone,
  pick each one and tick **Tick this one to merge**), then **Merge ...**.
  Pick the one to keep; horses, want lists and old page addresses move
  onto it.
- **Merge this mold into ...** (on the mold's header) when the same mold is
  listed twice. Pick the mold to keep; this mold's releases move under it
  and take its scale.
- A moderator's merge waits for a **second look**; the owner's merges
  don't (the owner is the second look). Every merge can be undone for 30
  days. Before the owner adds update 243, only the owner can merge, and the
  dialog says first that the merge **can't be undone** (its button reads
  **Merge for good**).
- **Remove** a row that nothing points at (no horses, want lists, set
  pieces or releases under it). It is hidden at once, the owner sees it in
  the daily digest, and any moderator can undo it for 30 days. If anything
  points at it, the Desk says so and offers a merge instead. Before the
  owner adds update 243 nothing can be removed, and the dialog says so
  before you type a reason.

Where a style rule is in effect, a **suggested fix** appears above the
fields with a **Use this** button. It fills the fields; you still check
them and save.

### To-do

Work lists found by scanning the whole Registry every hour, Stablemates
first. Each card says how many rows it has and who is on it:

| List                                              | What it means                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Model number in the title                         | "#5038 Morgan Mare": the number belongs in the number box.                                                                                                                                |
| No colour                                         | No colour description, or only "?". Fill in what you know.                                                                                                                                |
| Shop or sale as the title                         | "Sears Holiday Catalog" or "BreyerFest 1990 — Live Auction 01" as the name.                                                                                                               |
| Description in the title                          | Notes pasted into the name, like "(Replaced in 2006 by …)".                                                                                                                               |
| Molds with no generation tag                      | Breyer Stablemate molds without "(G1)", "(G2)" …                                                                                                                                          |
| Filed under the wrong scale                       | A release under a mold of another scale.                                                                                                                                                  |
| Possible duplicates or number clashes             | Identical releases, or two releases sharing a model number. Usually a wrong number, sometimes a real duplicate.                                                                           |
| Gift sets with missing pieces                     | A set names a horse that has no piece of that set filed under its mold.                                                                                                                   |
| Releases with no mold                             | Find the mold and file the release there.                                                                                                                                                 |
| Molds with no releases                            | Usually a duplicate mold, or its releases are filed elsewhere.                                                                                                                            |
| Typed mold name disagrees                         | The Mold box says one mold; the release is filed under another.                                                                                                                           |
| Names spelled two ways                            | Two maker or artist names a letter or two apart. A typo, or two people? Check first.                                                                                                      |
| Titles ending in punctuation / starting lowercase | Tidy-ups.                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Years stored as text                              | Saving that release stores a plain year as a number: press **Use this** on the suggested fix, or save any other change on it. A year written oddly ("1975-1988") waits for you to retype. |
| Colour in the title, Whole set in the title       | **Notes, not problems.** Some horses are named for their colour, and today's rule keeps a set's full list of horses in the title. These become work only if the moderators agree a rule.  |

- **I'm on it** holds a whole list, or one row, for a day, so two people
  don't fix the same rows. **Let it go** when you stop.
- **Done** takes a row off the list. At the next check, if the problem is
  still there, it comes back.
- **Check again** re-scans the Registry now (after a big batch of fixes).
- Clicking a row opens it in the Mold view, with that release picked, on
  **Needs work**, and **Back to the list** returns you to the same page of
  the list.

The same checks run in the owner's health scan
(`scripts/catalog-delta/registry_health.mjs`), so the numbers match.

### Rules

The Registry's **house style**, written by the moderators: how set pieces
are titled, what goes in run details, when a mold carries a generation
tag, and so on.

1. Anyone **proposes** a rule: a short name, the rule in a sentence or two,
   and (if one fits) the suggested fix the Desk should offer.
2. The other moderators read it and press **I agree** or **I disagree**
   (say why if you disagree).
3. At **3 agreements** it takes effect. The owner's agreement counts; your
   own doesn't count on a rule you proposed.
4. The owner can **veto** a rule at any time, with a note saying why, and
   can **retire** a rule that has done its job.

Until a rule is in effect, follow the Registry's current style (below).
You can propose up to 10 rules a day.

### History

Everything done on the Desk, newest first, with who did it and in which
role. Filter by who and by kind of change.

- **Undo** puts a change back, within 30 days, and any moderator can undo
  any change. Say why; the reason is kept. If someone has changed a field
  since, that field is left as it is and the Desk tells you. Right after a
  save or a removal, the message at the top of the page has its own
  **Undo** too.
- **Looks right** is a check-off: you have looked at another moderator's
  change and it is right. You can't check your own.
- Each line says where the change stands: **Done**, **Waiting for a second
  look**, **Second look by** someone, **Looks right**, **Declined** or
  **Undone**. **Open** opens the entry in the Desk's editor.

## The Registry's style today

Until the moderators agree otherwise on the Rules tab:

- **Titles hold the name only**, with a capital letter and no full stop at
  the end. The model number goes in its own box; a year may go at the end
  as "Name (2025)".
- **Colour descriptions start with a capital**: "Red chestnut, star".
- **One spelling per maker or artist.** If two spellings are the same
  person, merge into the more common one.
- **Sets**: one row per piece, each filed under the piece's own mold, with
  the full list of horses in the title.
- **Stablemate molds** carry their generation: "Saddlebred (G1)". The same
  breed at G1 and G4 is two different molds.
- **Unknown scale stays empty** on artist resins. Don't guess.
- **Scales, run types and materials** come from fixed lists. If one is
  missing, ask the owner; it can't be added from the Desk.

## Limits

Per moderator per day: 300 edits (approvals count), 20 merge requests,
50 removals and 10 rule proposals. More than 100 changes in an hour sends
the owner a note, just in case an account has been taken over. The owner
has no limits.

## What the owner sees

Once a day the owner gets a digest in the bell: how many changes were
made, and **every removal and every merge by name**, with who did it.
Merges, removals and limit hits also reach the owner straight away.

## What moderators don't do

Members' accounts, horses, shows, the market and the admin pages are not
part of the role. If you see a problem there, use the site's Report
button or the contact page, like any member.

## Stepping down

You can step down any time: tell the owner through the contact page.
Your changes stay, with your name on them.
