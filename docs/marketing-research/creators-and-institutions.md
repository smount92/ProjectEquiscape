### 2.3 NAMHSA & NAN

**What NAMHSA is:** the governing body for live model horse showing in North America. Founded **1994** by hobbyists online; first NAN held **1995** in Kentucky. A **501(c)(4)** governed by a Board, with a NAN Show Committee running the championship. `[HIGH]` https://namhsa.org/about-us/

**Membership:** individual membership **$30 USD for two years** (new and renewing the same). Confers full voting privileges, discounted NAN entry, and the ability to hold a NAN-qualifying show. Membership year runs **Jan 1 – Dec 31**. `[HIGH]` https://namhsa.org/become-a-member/ and https://namhsa.org/

**Show year:** **May 1 → April 30.** `[HIGH]` https://namhsa.org/become-a-member/

#### How NAN qualification actually works

1. A model earns qualification by placing **1st or 2nd in an OPEN class at a NAMHSA member show**. An "open class" = "competition where the entrant is not limited by skill level, age, or years of competition." `[HIGH]` https://namhsa.org/member-show-requirements/
2. Qualification is issued as a physical **"ticket," universally called a NAN card**, which is **the SOLE proof of qualification**. `[HIGH]` https://namhsa.org/entrants-tickets/
3. **Cards are colour-coded by category** — green = breed halter; yellow = collectibility/workmanship; pink/red = Western performance; separate cards for English performance and Other performance. `[MED]` — search snippet of the entrants page.
4. **One card per category per horse** may be submitted: up to one performance ticket per division (English / Western / Other), plus one Halter and/or one Non-Halter ticket. `[HIGH]` https://namhsa.org/entrants-tickets/
5. **Cards expire.** "Any NAN-Qualifying Show held after May 1 in a given year will qualify models to enter in the following **4 years** of NANs." `[HIGH]` https://namhsa.org/become-a-member/ (Note: the NAN page separately says cards "bearing years 2023-2029" are currently valid, and that 2019 cards were extended through 2022 because of pandemic cancellations. `[MED]` https://namhsa.org/north-american-nationals/ , https://namhsa.org/nan-faq/)
6. **Cards are consumed on use** — "Once a ticket is used, that horse must re-qualify for the next NAN, unless you possess multiple tickets for that horse." `[HIGH]`
7. **Cards cannot be replaced** if lost or damaged, and NAN cannot be entered without them. `[HIGH]`
8. **Cutoff:** the last date to enter a NAMHSA member show counting toward a given year's NAN is **April 30 of that year**. `[HIGH]`
9. **Cards must be filled in completely** (model name, class name, owner name). NAMHSA states that in previous years **"nearly a third of all NAN cards submitted for NAN entry were either incompletely or incorrectly filled out, or were expired,"** and such cards are revoked with no refund and no chance to correct. `[HIGH]` https://namhsa.org/nan-faq/

> **This is a screaming product opportunity and it is sourced, not inferred:** the hobby's championship qualification runs on **physical, irreplaceable, hand-written, expiring paper cards, and a third of them are submitted wrong.** Any digital tracking of NAN cards addresses a documented, quantified, officially-acknowledged pain point.

#### Becoming a NAN-qualifying show (relevant if the product ever hosts shows)

- **"Any LIVE model horse show shall be eligible for NAN-Qualifying Show status provided all requirements are met."** `[HIGH]` — exact quote https://namhsa.org/become-a-member/
- Apply and pay **≥60 days before the show**. Must not be "by invitation" — all persons must be allowed to enter. Must be publicly advertised in print or online in advance. Results due **within 30 days** of the show date. `[HIGH]` https://namhsa.org/member-show-requirements/
- **Card fee formula:** `(# of Classes) × (# of Judges) × (2 Cards per Class)`. Tiers: up to 250 cards $30; 251–500 $40; 501–750 $55; 751–1,000 $70; over 1,000 $70 + $7 per additional 100. `[HIGH]` https://namhsa.org/member-show-application/

## 3. PHOTO SHOWING — how it actually works in 2026

**This is the section most directly competitive with a photo-show product.**

### 3.1 The headline rule: photo shows do NOT qualify horses for NAN

**Sourced, exact quote:** *"Any **live** model horse show shall be eligible for NAN-Qualifying Show status provided all requirements are met."* `[HIGH]` https://namhsa.org/become-a-member/

NAMHSA's show-requirements and show-application pages contain **no** provision for photo, online, or virtual shows. `[HIGH]` https://namhsa.org/member-show-requirements/ , https://namhsa.org/member-show-application/

Wikipedia is explicit that it doesn't say either way. `[HIGH]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_horse_showing

> **Conclusion `[HIGH]`: photo shows cannot award NAN cards. Live shows only.** One historical asterisk: **NAN 2020 was itself held virtually** (98 entrants) `[HIGH]` https://namhsa.org/nan-archives/ — so NAMHSA has run a virtual national championship once, under duress. There is precedent, but no standing policy.
>
> **Marketing implication:** never imply a photo show on the platform earns NAN cards. That would be a factual error the hobby's core would catch instantly and it would be reputationally expensive. The honest positioning is "NAMHSA-style classes," not "NAN-qualifying."

### 3.2 How photo showing works

- Entrants photograph models against backgrounds and submit with the horse's recorded vitals ("stats"). Judges place on **quality of the photo, accuracy of breed depiction, and condition of the model** — same criteria as live shows, **plus** photo quality, framing, lighting, and how well the image represents the horse. `[HIGH]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_horse and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_horse_showing
- Main appeal: no transport risk, no travel cost, and **the same photo can be entered into multiple shows simultaneously**. `[MED]`
- Divisions mirror live showing: Halter (breed representation), Performance (pose, suitability, tack), plus Collectibility and Workmanship. `[HIGH]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_horse_showing
- **Postal showing** still exists, now mostly for performance disciplines mirroring real events (racing, dressage), historically using dice rolls. `[MED]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_horse

### 3.5 MEPSA — the establishment photo-show body

- **Model Equine Photo Showers Association**, formed **1997**, "a grass roots organization of model horse hobbyists... to promote the hobby of model horse photo showing on an International level." `[HIGH]` https://mepsa.club/about/
- **Strictly mail-in. Online entries are not accepted.** `[HIGH]` https://mepsa.club/ — this is MEPSA's defining constraint and its biggest vulnerability.
- Physical photo requirements: **4×6 preferred**; 4×5.25 and 3×5 allowed; other sizes may be disqualified. Class numbers written on the back of the photo. Payment by cash, cheque, PayPal, or show coupons mailed with the entries. `[HIGH]` https://mepsa.club/faqs/
- **Divisions by model size:** Traditional, Classic, Mini, Stablemate, Micro; classes by breed, gender, colour, and workmanship. A model may be shown in every section. `[HIGH]` same.
- **Qualification → championship:** models placing **1st–3rd** (1st–6th in classes of 100+) at monthly qualifiers qualify for the **year-end MEPSA Championship Show**. Described as "for photo showers, the equivalent of the North American Nationals... only on a world-wide level of competition." `[MED]` https://mepsa.club/international-championship-show/
- **Calendar structure — a strict monthly rhythm** `[HIGH]` https://mepsa.club/showing-judging/upcoming-shows/ (page noted "updated 7/28/2026"):
  - 1st Saturdays — OF Plastic Regular Qualifier (Div A)
  - 2nd Saturdays — OF China/AR/CM Regular Qualifier (Div B, C, D)
  - 3rd Saturdays — All Performance Regular Qualifier (Div E, F, I)
  - 4th Saturdays — Mini Qualifier (Div G, H)
  - → **12 monthly cycles × 4 qualifiers = ~48 regular shows/year**, plus specialties.
- **Published specialty & championship dates (2026–27)** `[HIGH]` same page:
  - **Oct 31, 2026** — Vintage Specialty
  - **Jan 30, 2027** — Classic Specialty
  - **May 15, 2027** — Foal Specialty
  - **Jun 26, 2027** — **International Championship Show**
  - **Jul 10, 2027** — Other Animal Show
  - (Championship is historically held in June `[MED]`)
