# Help with a deal that went wrong

**The site keeps the record; it does not referee and never holds money.**
Payment happens between members, outside the site.

## What exists

- A Deal Room thread holds offers, the signed terms boxes, and the installment ledger.
- Either party can **raise a dispute**: the deal freezes, the record is preserved, the other party is notified. Only the raiser can stand it down.
- The **evidence pack** is the frozen record both sides can take to their payment provider (PayPal buyer protection, a card chargeback) or elsewhere.
- **The admin is not notified of disputes** today. They reach the owner by contact form or message.

## Steps

1. **Read the deal record**: the signed terms, the ledger, the dates. Facts only.
2. **Tell the member what the record shows** and how to use the evidence pack with their payment provider. Do not decide who is right.
3. **Check for conduct** that breaks the site rules (threats, a pattern of non-delivery across several deals, a fake listing). That part is `moderation-report`.
4. **Never** move money, promise a refund, or say the site guarantees a sale.
5. Draft the reply; the owner sends it.
